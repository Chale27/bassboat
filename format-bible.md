# Bass Boat format bible (v1, from the top-50 Zack D. Films Shorts teardown, 2026-10-18)

Borrow the structure. Never copy art, characters, scripts, titles, music or footage.

## Every video
- Length 25-32 s. Narration 75-90 words at about 2.8 words/second.
- Subject on screen by 0.4 s. No logo, no title card first.
- 0-3 hard cuts. Prefer one continuous moving camera (push-in, orbit, pull-out) on one scene.
- Second-person narration ("you", "your").
- Captions: 2-4 words per card, white, bold, centered, kept above the bottom fifth of the frame (TikTok/Reels UI).
- End on the payoff. No outro, no "follow for more".
- Every factual sentence is cited in storyboard.json `claims` with a `source_id` from sources.json.
- Original music/sfx only (generated or licensed for use). No copyrighted footage or music.
- AI-generated label turned on when posting.

## Six opener types
1. "If you ..." conditional (what happens if you do X)
2. "This is what happens when ..." demonstration
3. Shocking number ("A bass can ...")
4. "You've been doing X wrong"
5. Body-inside reveal ("Inside a bass's ...")
6. Countdown/challenge ("10 facts in under 30 seconds")

## Format A: "What happens if" (5 timed beats)
1. 0-3 s   Hook: the "if you..." setup, subject already moving.
2. 3-9 s   Mechanism: what starts happening inside/around the fish.
3. 9-17 s  Escalation: the consequence gets worse; camera moves closer or into a cutaway.
4. 17-26 s Turn: the surprising detail or the fix.
5. 26-30 s Payoff: the last line lands the answer. Cut to black on the last word.

## Format B: "10 bass facts in under 30 seconds"
- On-screen counter 1-10 and a running stopwatch from 0.0 s.
- About 2.5 s per fact, 7-9 words each. One visual per fact.
- Ends on the question "Which one did you already know?" (drives comments).

## House styles (pick two after the 5-second tests)
- clay: soft clay-like 3D, warm light, rounded shapes.
- blueprint: navy background, white/cyan line art, labeled callouts.
- isometric: isometric underwater cross-section, flat colors, depth layers.
